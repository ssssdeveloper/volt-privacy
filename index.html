VOLT — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026 · Applies to the Android app “VOLT” (com.ssssdev.volt). A browser build exists only as a developer preview and is not publicly distributed.

Summary

VOLT is an arcade game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and it never asks for your name, email, contacts, location, or photos. The app collects pseudonymous gameplay statistics to improve the game, and shows advertisements served by Google AdMob.

Who is responsible

The data controller is ssss developer, the independent developer who publishes VOLT on Google Play (the developer identity shown on the app's store listing). Contact: ssssdeveloperssss@gmail.com; a postal address is available on request through that address.

Data the app collects

The app records gameplay events — such as runs played, scores, session length, taps, in-game unlocks, and whether advertisements were offered, watched, or skipped — together with basic device context (screen size, platform, OS version, app version and update build, and a yes/no flag indicating whether a screen reader or other accessibility service is active, used only to interpret input statistics correctly). These events are associated with a randomly generated install identifier created on first launch. This identifier is not linked to your identity, and we have no way to connect it to you personally.

To deliver app updates, the app contacts Expo's update service on launch and sends a randomly generated update-client identifier together with the app version, runtime version and platform; like any internet request, Expo's servers also receive network metadata such as your IP address for the duration of the request.

Advertising

VOLT shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data — including your device’s advertising ID — to serve and measure ads, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data practices. Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), the app shows a consent form before serving personalized ads, and you can choose non-personalized ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Google settings at any time.

Legal bases

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process pseudonymous gameplay statistics and update checks on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding, improving, and maintaining the game (Art. 6(1)(f)); you can object at any time by contacting us or by uninstalling the app, which stops all collection. Personalized advertising in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), collected and changeable through the in-app consent form; declining leaves the game fully playable with non-personalized or no advertising.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and held by our providers under their access controls. The app collects no passwords, payment details, contacts, precise location, or other sensitive data, and it opts out of Android's cloud backup for its local data.

Data sharing

Gameplay statistics are sent to and processed by PostHog, Inc. (privacy policy), an analytics provider acting on our behalf, on servers located in the United States. Advertising data is processed by Google and its ad technology partners. The app also contacts Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) (privacy policy) to check for and download app updates; that request carries basic device and app-version information. Because these providers operate in the United States, your data may be processed outside your country of residence. For transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland we rely on the provider's certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider holds one, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR); you can obtain a copy of the applicable safeguard by contacting us at the address below. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share data with anyone else.

Data retention and deletion

Gameplay statistics are retained for no longer than 24 months from collection, after which they are deleted or irreversibly aggregated. Data stored on your device (scores, unlocks, settings, buffered statistics) is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage; the app opts out of Android's cloud backup so this data is not restored on reinstall. To request deletion of analytics data tied to your install identifier, contact us at the address below and include your install identifier: open the “?” menu on the app's title screen, press and hold the “ID …” line to copy it, and paste it into your email.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because the app holds no account, requests are matched using your install identifier. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the advertising consent form shown in the app lets you grant or withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time; you can reopen it from the “Privacy options” item under the “?” menu on the title screen whenever it applies to you.

Children

VOLT is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains no chat, no social features, and no free-text input of any kind.
