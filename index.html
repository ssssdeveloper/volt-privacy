VOLT — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026 · Applies to the Android app “VOLT” (com.ssssdev.volt)

Summary

VOLT is an arcade game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and it never asks for your name, email, contacts, location, or photos. The app collects pseudonymous gameplay statistics to improve the game, and shows advertisements served by Google AdMob.

Data the app collects

The app records gameplay events — such as runs played, scores, session length, taps, in-game unlocks, and whether advertisements were offered, watched, or skipped — together with basic device context (screen size, platform, OS version, app version). These events are associated with a randomly generated install identifier created on first launch. This identifier is not linked to your identity, and we have no way to connect it to you personally.

Advertising

VOLT shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data — including your device’s advertising ID — to serve and measure ads, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s data practices. Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), the app shows a consent form before serving personalized ads, and you can choose non-personalized ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Google settings at any time.

Data sharing

Gameplay statistics may be processed by analytics infrastructure acting on our behalf. Advertising data is processed by Google and its ad technology partners. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share data with anyone else.

Data retention and deletion

Gameplay statistics are retained only as long as needed to analyze and improve the game. Data stored on your device (scores, unlocks, settings, buffered statistics) is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. To request deletion of analytics data tied to your install identifier, contact us at the address below.

Children

VOLT is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains no chat, no social features, and no free-text input of any kind.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
